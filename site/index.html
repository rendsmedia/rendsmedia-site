Independent visual audits for donors

Reports tell you what was done. We show you whether it happened. Our Continuous Implementation Monitoring service sends an independent team to your grantees' project sites to document them as they are.

Audits are agreed with you in advance and written into the grant, so field visits are expected, cleared with local authorities and carried out with full consent from the people we film.

Request a sample audit brief
  1. Dispatch within 72 hoursA field team reaches the project site on short notice.
  2. GPS- and time-stamped photo logEvery image carries its location and capture time, so old or reused photos can't pass as new.
  3. Verified beneficiary interviewsShort, consented interviews with the people a project is meant to serve.
  4. Executive audit briefA concise written summary of what we found, with the visual evidence attached.

How an assignment runs

The same four stages whether it's a one-day event or a month in the field.

  1. Understand

    We learn what the material is for, who will see it and what it must prove.

  2. Plan

    Locations, permissions, consent, transport and a shot list, agreed before anyone travels.

  3. Produce

    A crew sized to the job, working quietly and treating every person on camera with respect.

  4. Deliver

    Edited files in the formats you need, on the date we agreed, with raw material archived.
